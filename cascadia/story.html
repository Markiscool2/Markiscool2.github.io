◆ Our Story

A quieter way to buy exceptional cars.

Cascadia Collection began with a simple observation: the finest automobiles in the Pacific Northwest were being sold the same way as any other used car — volume-focused, hurried, transactional. We wanted to do it differently.

We opened a private showroom in Enumclaw in 2022 — close enough to Seattle to reach in under an hour, remote enough to feel like you've left the city behind. Our clients fly in from across the country, drive up from Portland, or simply block a weekday morning. There is no sales floor. There is a room with seven cars, a table, coffee, and however long it takes.

Every vehicle we offer passes through a disciplined intake: history, service records, accident and ownership verification, independent inspection, and a road evaluation. Where provenance is uncertain, we pass. Where it is extraordinary, we document it fully.

We are small by design. Seven cars at a time means we can know each one deeply — the history of every panel, the story of every owner, the specifics of every service entry. If you want to buy a supercar the way you would buy a watch or a painting — with real time, real information, and no pressure — you are in the right place.

— The Cascadia Collection team
