◆ Consignment & Direct Purchase

Sell your car quietly.

We work with owners who would rather not post a six-figure automobile to the open market. Whether you want a direct offer today or a discrete consignment to our network of known buyers, we handle the process end-to-end — photography, marketing, negotiation, transport, and closing paperwork.

I / Direct Offer

A cash offer

Send us the details and photos. If it fits the collection, we'll make a firm offer within 48 hours — funds wired at pickup. No inspection surprises, no back-and-forth.

II / Consignment

Discrete placement

We photograph, write, and market to our network — often without a public listing. Typical placement: 2–8 weeks for well-specified examples. You keep the car until it sells.

III / Trade

Trade upward

Rolling into something from our inventory? We credit your trade against the new car and manage both transactions in one close. One wire, one signature.

Tell us about your car

We respond within the business day. For fastest response, include year, make, model, trim, mileage, and a few photos.
