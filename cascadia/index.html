Philosophy

Cascadia Collection was founded on a simple premise: that the finest automobiles deserve to be considered the way a gallery considers its works. Not inventoried. Not flipped. Considered.

We operate from a private showroom in the foothills east of Seattle — close enough to reach, remote enough to feel away. Our clients fly in, drive up, or simply request a quiet hour on a weekday morning. There is no sales floor. There is a room with seven cars in it, a table, coffee, and however long it takes.

Every vehicle passes through a disciplined intake: history, service records, accident and ownership verification, independent inspection, and a road evaluation. Where provenance is uncertain, we pass. Where it is extraordinary, we document it fully.

Services
Full-Spectrum

Beyond the sale.

Acquisition is the beginning. We support clients across the full arc of ownership — consignment, sourcing, transport, storage, and the quiet logistics of a collection that travels.

I / Acquire

Private purchase

Every viewing is private and unhurried. We structure purchase, delivery, and title work across state and international lines, with full transparency on condition, history, and market context. Financing available through our partners.

II / Consign

Discrete consignment

If you would rather not post your car to the open market, we offer consignment with full discretion. We photograph, write, market to our network of known buyers, and close — often without the car ever being publicly listed.

III / Source

Bespoke sourcing

Looking for a specific example — a matching-numbers 550 Maranello, a low-mile Carrera GT, a particular spec of 765LT? Tell us what you are building toward. We will find it, inspect it, and bring it to you when it is right.
